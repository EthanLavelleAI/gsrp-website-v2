Departments

Our Departments

Georgia State Roleplay offers a variety of departments to join. We are not accepting any other departments as we do not need any others. Department opportunities may be re-opened in the future.

DOJ

Department of Justice

Oversee legal proceedings, prosecute cases, and uphold justice across the state of Georgia.

FBI

Federal Bureau of Investigation

Conduct federal-level investigations, counter-terrorism operations, and major criminal cases.

GSP

Georgia State Patrol

Patrol state highways, enforce traffic laws, and ensure safety across statewide jurisdictions.

FCSO

Fulton County Sheriff's Office

Serve and protect the residents of Fulton County through local law enforcement operations.

AFD

Atlanta Fire Department

Respond to fire emergencies, provide rescue services, and protect the community of Atlanta.
