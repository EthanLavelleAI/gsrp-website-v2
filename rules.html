Guidelines

Server Rules

Follow these in-game rules to maintain a professional and immersive roleplay environment. Violations can result in warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal.

01

Adhere to Terms of Service

Always follow the Roblox and GSRP Terms of Service. It's the foundation of our community.

02

Use Common Sense

Common sense is crucial. Our moderators have the right to remove you from a roleplay if your actions are unreasonable.

03

No Random Deathmatch (RDM)

Intentionally harming another player without a reasonable in-game cause is strictly forbidden. This keeps the roleplay fair and immersive.

04

No Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

Don't intentionally ram, shoot, or push another player's vehicle. It's a quick way to get removed from a scene.

05

New Life Rule (NLR)

If you are killed or removed by a moderator, you cannot return to that scene. This applies even if a panic button is pressed or your mafia requests your presence.

06

Do Not Interfere with Roleplays

Stay out of scenes you weren't part of from the start. Respect marked-off areas (like those with cones or tape) to avoid disrupting others.

07

Realistic Avatars Required

Your avatar should look like a real person. No wings, tails, antlers, or similar accessories are allowed. For any animal roleplay, you must have permission from a SHR+ staff member.

08

Activity Permissions

Hostages, Roadworks, and Checkpoints require permission from an on-duty staff member before you can continue them.

09

Safe Zones

Shooting is strictly prohibited in: All Civilian Spawns, All Fire Department Spawns, Sheriff/Police Spawn, DOT Spawn, Court House, and Both Gun Stores.

10

Banned Weapons

Civilian: M249 (Booster/VIP Only), Remington MSR (Banned), Remington 700 (Banned)

Law Enforcement: Benelli M4 (Banned)

11

Vehicle Regulations

Civilian Vehicles: Strugatti Ettore 2020 (Booster Only), Chevlon Corbeta RZR (Booster Only), Chevlon Corbeta 8 2023 (Booster Only), Pea Car 2025 (Banned), Lawn Mower (Banned), Canyon Descender (Banned), 4-Wheeler (Banned)

Job Vehicles: Metro Transit Bus (Banned), Forklift (Banned), Front Loader Tractor (Banned)

Law Enforcement: Prisoner Transport Bus (Departmental use only), Canyon Descender (Banned)
